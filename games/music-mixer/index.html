Touch spread is how far past a pad's edge a finger still counts. Raise it and one fingertip laid across a seam fires both pads — four fingers on the four inner crossings of two columns hold all ten.

HOW IT WORKS

Every pad is one track of the song. The transport runs whether or not you are holding anything, so tracks always come in on the beat and in sync. Hold all fifteen and you hear the whole arrangement.

The 🔓 toggle at the end of the song row switches how a pad answers. Off, a track sounds only while you hold it. On, a tap turns a pad on and leaves it on until you tap it again. Switching lock off does not release what is already locked — tap a locked pad to let it go. ↻ restarts the song and clears everything.

A held pad pulses on each of its own notes. Lit but not pulsing means that track is resting in this section of the song.

LAYOUT

Hue runs red to magenta up the left column, then the middle, then the right. The right-hand column is the percussion — low drum at the bottom, metal at the top. Left column is the low end, middle column is harmony and melody. Rotate to landscape and the same three groups become rows, percussion on top.

RECORDING

Pick RECORD NEW SONG at the top of the song list and the pads stop being a mixer and become instruments: the two left columns are ten notes of a scale climbing from the red pad, and the right column stays percussion. The 🔧 button beside the song list is where the rest of it lives.

Under TAP PAD, a quick tap on a single pad opens its sound. Chords, holds and slides still play normally, so you can keep playing in that view.
