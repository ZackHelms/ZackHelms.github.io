HOW TO FIGHT

The game pauses while this menu is open.

Move with the stick at the bottom left: put a thumb down anywhere in the lower left and push. You always face your opponent; up walks at them, sideways circles them.

Attack (the big button): tap again as each cut lands and the cuts chain into a five-cut combo (diagonal, rising, horizontal, thrust, overhead), then the chain starts over. Stop tapping and the chain ends.

Parry (above attack): tap it just before an enemy cut lands. Caught in the short window, the cut is turned aside and your opponent is staggered: while they stagger they cannot parry or dodge, and every cut you land does 10% more damage. Mistimed, a parry leaves you open for a moment.

Dodge (left of attack): a quick dash the way the stick points, or backwards with the stick at rest. For a split second in the middle of it you cannot be hit, so a well-timed dodge can go straight through a cut.

Energy: a dodge that slips a cut, a parry, and the third and fifth cut of an unbroken combo each give 1 point. The ring round the power attack button fills with it; at 10 it closes and flashes and the power attack is ready. It costs 10, and you can bank 20 to throw two back to back. A power attack (yours or your opponent's) cannot be parried, only dodged, and a fighter winding one up does not flinch when hit.

Keyboard: WASD or the arrow keys to move, J attack, K dodge, L parry, I power attack, Esc for this menu.

Performance stats: FPS is frames a second; frame is the time between frames (average and slowest in the last half second); CPU is the page's own time a frame (fight, 3D, HUD); calls and tris are draw calls and triangles a frame.
