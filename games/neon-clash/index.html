ℹ HOW CARDS WORK
FIELD MANUAL
TANK 4
Unit. High HP, low damage, average speed. A wall that soaks hits while the rest of your push works.
FIGHTER 3
Unit. Average HP and damage, fast attacks, fast legs. Runs down archers before they can whittle you.
ARCHER 3
Unit. Low HP, average damage, average speed — but shoots from far back. Melts anything that cannot reach it.
BUNKER 8
Building. Stationary, holds two units. Aim it at your emplacement, an edge, or a bunker already standing there and it slides to the nearest ground it fits rather than being refused. Garrisoned units stop moving, fire through the slits, and take no damage — the bunker eats it. Two per side; blow one up and it can be rebuilt.
FIREBALL 5
Spell. Lobbed from your base and two seconds in the air — so lead a moving target, or drop it on a push that has already committed and stopped to grind. An airburst on your own half. Damage and knockback are hardest at the centre and halve at the rim, so it pays against a crowd and barely dents one unit — the card to hold when a big push is coming. Scatters what it hits; bases are immune, and a garrison inside a bunker is shielded from it.
BASE
The emplacement at the back of each half. Reduce the enemy base to zero and the match is yours — but it is not a sitting target: its turret has an archer's reach and cadence and hits for two archers a shot, so a lone unit that walks in alone dies to the base itself.
SIEGE
The moment a unit lands its first hit on a building — a bunker or a base — it commits to that target until the building falls, and a dashed tether marks it. A committed unit will not turn on your defenders while it grinds, so answering a push that has already reached your base is the cheapest fighting you will ever do. Units still only marching at you can be pulled off.
DEPLOY
Two ways, both fine: drag a card onto the board, or tap a card to arm it and then tap where you want it. Aim at the enemy half and it simply lands on your side of the halfway line at the same across-the-board spot; aim a building somewhere it cannot stand and it slides to the nearest spot it can. Either way you never lose the card to a bad aim. Release over the tray to think again.
SUDDEN DEATH
At 3:00 both sides start earning +1 extra energy a second, and another +1 every minute after — up to 8/sec, where a full bar refills in under three seconds. The music picks up with it. Nobody gets to sit on a lead; at 10:00 the match is called on remaining base HP.
STYLE
The cogwheel in the top left has a dropdown that switches the whole arena between TOON (the default) and NEON at any time, mid-match included. It is paint only — every stat, range and rule is identical in both.
