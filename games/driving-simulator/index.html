HOW TO DRIVE

The game pauses while this menu is open.

Steering wheel (bottom left): put a finger on the rim and turn it. Let go and it centres itself. Tap the middle of it to sound the horn.

Pedals (bottom right): the tall one is the accelerator and the wide one is the brake. To back up, brake to a stop and keep holding the brake: after a second at 0 mph the car goes into reverse, the brake pedal drives it backwards and the wheel steers as it would in a real car. The accelerator puts it back in drive.

Cruise control (the button above the accelerator): tap it at speed and the car holds that speed. Press the accelerator to go faster for a while; let go and it settles back. Braking, reversing or a crash switches it off, and so does another tap.

Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD to steer and drive, Space to brake, H for the horn, X for cruise control, C to change the view, Esc to close this menu.

Performance stats: FPS is frames a second. Frame is the time between frames (average and slowest in the last half second). CPU is the time the page's own code takes a frame: sim (driving, traffic, building the road ahead), 3D (preparing the picture) and HUD. GPU is the graphics chip's time, where the browser reports it. Calls and tris are draw calls and triangles a frame; res is the size of the 3D picture in pixels; chunks are loaded 96 m pieces of road, then cars, lights and memory where the browser reports it.

Views: the car button at the top right goes from the driver's seat to behind the car to a free orbit you can drag round and pinch in or out. In the driver's seat, drag across the windscreen to look round.

THE ROAD

An endless two-way US road, keep right. It runs through four kinds of road, each with its own speed limit: a rural two-lane (55 mph, dashed yellow centre line), a town two-lane (35 mph, kerbs, sidewalks and streetlights), a four-lane arterial (45 mph, double yellow) and a six-lane highway (65 mph). Lanes come and go on tapers as long as the federal rule asks for (lane width times speed), signed as the right lane ends.

Lane widths, line widths, the 10 ft dash on a 40 ft cycle, the cars' sizes and the colours they are painted in come from zmh-3d's road kit; the houses, shops, offices and trees come from its town kit.

The sky comes from zmh-sky: the sun, the moon, the planets and the stars, computed for Raleigh, NC and the game's date and time.
