Map

Tap a square to walk there. Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom. Tap a door, a thing or a person to see it. Arrow keys and WASD also walk; Q E Z C step diagonally.

Roll initiative to count rounds: everyone in the fight takes a turn in order, with 30 ft of movement, one action and one bonus action. Tap an enemy in reach to strike it; tap your own piece for what else it can do. Enter ends a turn.

Game Master

The Game Master is Claude, speaking through your own key and at your own cost. The key stays in this browser, is sent only to api.anthropic.com, and is never written into a save. Without one the board still plays: walking, fighting and resting are the engine's, and free.

The beats: the opening, the first sight of a new place, a fight starting and ending. Walking, doors and looking at things are always silent and free.
