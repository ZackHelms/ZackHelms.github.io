ANIMATION RIGS

A001

Eight stick-figure rigs live in a grey-box course: two flights of stairs over a platform, and a ladder up a tower with a big red button on top. Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom, two fingers (or right-drag) to pan, tap a figure to follow it.

The rig: 21 joints, bone lengths fixed, every arm and leg solved each frame with two-bone inverse kinematics. Walking: feet are planted in the world and only move in their swing, landing where the body will be at mid-stance; on stairs one tread per step. The ladder: limbs climb in diagonal pairs, with keyed moves to get on, onto the tower and off.

The button: whoever reaches the top presses it and the flights fold into slides; anyone on them falls, slides down and gets up. The slides fold back a few seconds later, never under a rider, and the button only works (and glows) while the flights are stairs. One climber at a time: someone always waits at the foot of the ladder to go next.

Rendering: no 3D library, a hand-written perspective projection onto a 2D canvas, faces tiled and drawn far to near, shadows projected from one sun.

A002

Added the speed and version menus. Every older version stays exactly as it shipped; ?v=A001 on the address opens on one.

A body-mass model: fourteen segments with their share of the mass (head and neck 8.1%, trunk 49.7%, upper arm 2.8%, forearm 1.6%, hand 0.6%, thigh 10%, shank 4.65%, foot 1.45%; source: Winter, Biomechanics and Motor Control of Human Movement, after Dempster). The whole-body centre of mass is computed from the pose every frame and kept over the support by leaning the trunk: forward up stairs and when speeding up, the hips back when reaching for the button. Weight shifts side to side onto the loaded foot, the swing hip drops, the pelvis bobs, figures breathe and shift weight while standing.

Smoothing, after legs and feet were seen to snap: each swing runs on its own clock, landings glide and commit before touch-down, feet follow the stair profile over each riser, knees and elbows ease toward straight, and every follower is a damped spring.

A003

Fixed A002's walk on flat ground (short, rapid steps and a lean back): normal ~0.7 m steps at a normal pace. Standing balance moved to the hips and ankles, trunk upright.

Shadows on the stairs now glide down the flight instead of jumping a step per tread.

A004 (default)

A physics engine under the gait, built on A002's segment masses. The trunk is a rigid body hinged at the hips with the mass and inertia of its segments: gravity tips it, the pelvis's acceleration drags it, the hip muscles hold it, so it lags, overshoots and settles like a mass. The legs push the body up but can never pull it down faster than it would fall, and never reach further than they are long.

Balance is the force law: the ground pushes from under the feet through the centre of mass, and its forward part is what accelerates the body, so the mass sits ahead of the feet by height × acceleration ÷ gravity. A figure starting to run leans forward while it speeds up and straightens once it is at speed; it brakes with its feet, trunk up; it leans into turns; up a flight its mass rides over the upper foot. Before, A001 to A003 bent the trunk to put the mass there, so slowing into the stairs leaned it back and speeding up at the top leaned it forward.

Stairs without the squat: climbed on nearly straight legs, the trailing foot rising onto its toes; a foot that cannot reach the tread flat lands on the ball of the foot. Going down, the standing leg bends to lower the body. The plan each figure follows now asks only for what feet can deliver: accelerations ramped in and out, strides that lengthen with speed, slowing before a sharp turn or a flight.

Not simulated: the fall-and-catch inside each step. A draft that did it fell off the flights whenever a step could not catch it.
