What goes into these two numbers
  • Driving, both ends: 13,200 mi/yr across two cars (~6,600 each). assumed
  • High end: 22 mpg cars (0.4 kg CO2/mi), ~7,200 kWh grid electricity, gas furnace and gas water heater (~63 MMBtu/yr). This is the reference page's baseline plus the second car's driving. source: EPA, EIA RECS inferred
  • Low end: 50 mpg hybrids (not plug-in). House goes all-electric with a heat pump (~12,400 kWh/yr), fully covered by rooftop solar at 41 g CO2/kWh lifecycle. assumed solar factor: IPCC median via Wikipedia
  • Low end battery: one 13.5 kWh home battery at ~75 kg CO2 per kWh to build (published range 40-150), spread over 12 years = ~0.23 kg/day. inferred
  • Tap water, both ends: ~300 gal (1,136 L) per day, the EPA average for a family at home. source: EPA WaterSense

How each number is calculated
  • CO2: grid electricity and data centers 0.35 kg/kWh (US average); rooftop solar 0.041 kg/kWh; gasoline 8.89 kg/gal; natural gas 53 kg/MMBtu. source: EIA, EPA, IPCC
  • Energy: energy used at the point of use (your meter, your gas tank, the data center). Gasoline counted at 33.7 kWh/gal. source: EPA MPGe basis
  • Water: water consumed at home plus upstream. Grid electricity ~4.35 L/kWh at power plants (LBNL; USGS-based estimates are about half). Data centers add ~0.37 L/kWh onsite cooling. Gasoline ~19 L/gal for extraction and refining (Argonne: 2.1-5.4 gal per gal of oil produced plus ~1.5 gal refining). Solar and natural gas treated as ~0. inferred
  • Heat: nearly all energy ends up as heat. Grid electricity counts power-plant losses using the EPA source-to-site ratio of 2.80; onsite solar 1.00; natural gas 1.05; gasoline 1.0. One "kettle" = heating 1.5 L of water from 20C to boiling = 0.14 kWh. source: ENERGY STAR Portfolio Manager kettle inferred
  • Entropy: heat released divided by room temperature (293 K). A simple physics estimate of the entropy added to the environment when useful energy turns into waste heat. inferred
  • AI sessions: a 30-min Claude Opus extra-high session is assumed to cost $3-15 in API-equivalent compute, converted at 27-129 Wh per dollar from Hausfather's logged Claude Code usage. Central estimate ~0.6 kWh, which matches Hausfather's median heavy session. Anthropic publishes no per-token energy data. assumed inferred
