      HOW THIS WAS BUILT READ BEFORE TRUSTING A COLOR

      Candidate list. Taken from the NC State Board of Elections general-election candidate list (PDF dated 2026-09-21, plus the statewide candidate CSV), filtered to contests on Wake County ballots. Names are spelled exactly as they appear on the ballot.

      Which contests reach 27513. A ZIP code is not a voting district. I aggregated the NCSBE Wake County voter file (2026-09-20) by residential ZIP: all 34,700 active and inactive voters in 27513 were counted by U.S. House, NC Senate, NC House, commissioner, school-board, superior-court and district-court district. Only the counts were kept. That is how the page knows, for example, that 27513 splits between U.S. House NC-2 (77%) and NC-4 (23%), and that about 5% of 27513 (commissioner District 4) has no district commissioner race this year. Precinct 05-05 (3 voters in 27513, the rest in 27617) is left out of the picker.

      Your exact ballot. Pick your precinct above. Precinct 04-10 is split between NC-2 and NC-4, so if you live there, check your sample ballot with the NCSBE Voter Search link.

      How the colors were assigned. Each color is my reading of the candidate's public statements, votes and bill sponsorships, using your definitions:

      • Green needs support for AI or data centers AND conditions that make them pay their own way or leave the community better off (own power, no ratepayer subsidy, no tax breaks, local benefits, local say).
      • Yellow covers mixed or contradictory positions, for example strong pay-your-own-way terms combined with support for tax breaks.
      • Orange is opposition (or support for pauses) with no stated terms for yes. Red is support with no stated protections. No candidate currently rates red.
      • Gray is not a rating. It means nothing public was found, or the office (judges, sheriff, clerk, DA) does not set AI or data-center policy. Most down-ballot candidates are gray.
      • "Inferred" means the color comes from a bill co-sponsorship or vote, not the candidate's own words. Confidence describes how sure I am of the color, not anything about the person.

      Limits. Research was done through web search. The research environment blocked direct page loads, so a few campaign-site links were derived from the email domain on the candidate's NCSBE filing and are marked (unverified). Quotes come from the news sources linked beside them. Some sources are partisan (party committees, Cardinal Pine, Breitbart, NRSC); they are labeled where used.
